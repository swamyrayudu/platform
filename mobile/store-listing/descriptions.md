## Full description (limit 4000)

Prepare for AP DSC SGT the way the real exam works. RSD Education gives you syllabus-mapped practice questions, full-length mock tests under real exam timing, and clear progress reports, in both Telugu and English medium.

WHY RSD EDUCATION
• Built on the official exam pattern: mock tests follow the same structure, question count and time limit as the real paper.
• 100% syllabus-mapped: every question belongs to a subject and topic from the AP DSC SGT syllabus, so you always know what you are practising.
• Telugu and English medium: switch medium any time. Bilingual papers are clearly labelled.

PRACTICE BY SUBJECT AND TOPIC
• Choose a subject, pick the topics you want and start practising in seconds.
• Practice modes for every need: new questions you have not tried, questions you got wrong, a mix of topics and difficulty, or a focus on your weaker topics.
• See the answer and explanation after each question, or review everything at the end.
• Today's topics: let the app pick a balanced set of topics for your daily study.

FULL-LENGTH MOCK TESTS
• Exam-style papers with a real countdown timer, marking rules and sections.
• Question palette: jump to any question, see what you have answered, skipped or marked for review.
• Pause safely: stop the timer and save, then resume the paper later from where you left off.
• Mock modules unlock in sequence, so you build up step by step.

DETAILED RESULTS AND ANSWER REVIEW
• Marks, percentage, correct, wrong and skipped answers, time taken and accuracy at a glance.
• Section and topic scores show exactly where you are losing marks.
• Review every question with your answer, the correct answer, all options and the explanation.
• Filter the review by subject, or show only wrong, skipped or marked questions.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Progress reports by subject and topic, so you can see your strong and weak areas.
• History of all your practice sessions and mock tests.
• A 90-day exam roadmap with daily study notes: download them once and read them offline.

MAKE YOUR OWN TESTS
• Create a custom test from the subjects and topics you choose, and take it like a real mock.

SIMPLE AND SECURE

Who is it for?
Candidates preparing for the Andhra Pradesh DSC Secondary Grade Teacher (SGT) exam who want focused, exam-real practice in Telugu or English.
